Current

Today's site. Clay #c96442 appears only on hover and while recording. Hierarchy comes from serif size alone, and color only means valence.

A. Marker accent

A real pen used sparingly. Hand-drawn strokes go behind a few words that are about you or are the answer: the active nav item, your chosen “Good”, your score, “78% match”, the “you” marker, the words still being transcribed. Hover stays clay.

B. Interaction color

Yellow replaces clay for everything you touch: hover becomes a wash behind ink, the editable field you're in gets a wash, text selection is yellow, the comparison option you're on gets a wash and an amber edge, and recording gets a pulsing dot. At rest the page looks like Current.

C. Bolder

Yellow as the brand color. A solid wordmark, primary actions (Done, Next, Follow) as yellow pills, page titles underlined in yellow, a pale band across the ranker header, big scores on a yellow block, the chosen option filled solid.
